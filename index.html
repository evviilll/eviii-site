FAQ

Questions & answers

What does Eviii do?

Eviii is a Windows desktop app that exports and prepares your own videos for high-quality uploads to short-video platforms, in 1080p at up to 60 fps.

Which systems are supported?

Eviii runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).

How do I receive my license key?

After a successful payment, our Telegram bot @eviiUploudApp instantly sends you a personal license key. Paste it into the app to activate Premium.

Does Premium renew automatically?

No. Premium is a one-time payment for a fixed period (1 or 3 months). When it ends, the app returns to the free version until you buy a new period.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — if your key was not activated, or if a technical issue we can’t resolve prevents you from using Premium, you can request a refund within 7 days of purchase. See the Refund Policy.
